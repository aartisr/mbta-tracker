# Rider guide

## Find the next useful answer

Start with what you know:

- A route or line when you already know your service.
- A stop when you are at or near a station.
- An address or landmark when you need nearby transit.
- A vehicle when you have an identifier or direct reference.

The live experience is at [MBTA Tracker](https://mbta.ai-aarti.com/).

## Read realtime information responsibly

Look for freshness and connection state before making a time-critical choice. A displayed arrival is useful context, but it cannot remove upstream service changes or feed delays.

For a practical delay workflow, see [How to check MBTA delays](https://mbta.ai-aarti.com/guides/how-to-check-mbta-delays). For major incidents, use official MBTA alerts as the authoritative source.
